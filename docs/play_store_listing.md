# VeriMundi Play Store Listing Draft

## App Name

VeriMundi

## Short Description

Explore a fictional demo of global news discovery beyond the usual headlines.

## Full Description

VeriMundi is a demo MVP for exploring a broader, more transparent way to scan global developments. It highlights fictional story examples from countries and regions that are often underrepresented in major international feeds.

This release uses demo content only. Stories, sources, source counts, AI summaries, and AI answers are fictional and are provided for product evaluation. VeriMundi does not currently provide live news, breaking-news alerts, real source retrieval, user accounts, or real GenAI provider calls.

Current demo features:

- World dashboard with severity-aware country markers.
- World Pulse, Underreported, Positive World, and Saved story views.
- Story detail pages with mock AI-labeled summaries, confirmed facts, uncertainties, coverage comparison, and fictional source metadata.
- Transparent demo labels across the app.
- Local saved-story snapshots stored on the device.
- Light and dark Material 3 themes.

VeriMundi is designed around the principle that importance is not the same as popularity. This MVP demonstrates the interface and safety posture before live ingestion, translation, clustering, account sync, and production AI services are added.

- The app does not collect account data.
- The app does not use ads, in-app purchases, or user-generated content.
- The app does not call a commercial GenAI provider from the client.
- Saved stories are stored locally on the device.
