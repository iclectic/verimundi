# Play Console Checklist

Use this checklist for the VeriMundi Android demo MVP submission.

## App Setup

- Create the app in Play Console as an app, not a game.
- Use `VeriMundi` as the store display name.
- Confirm the package name is `com.iclectic.verimundi`.
- Add the production support email.
- Accept Play App Signing.
- Add the public privacy policy URL.

## Store Listing

- Use the copy in `docs/play_store_listing.md`.
- Upload screenshots for World, Underreported, Positive World, Story Detail, Saved, and Settings.
- Keep demo content labels visible in screenshots where practical.
- Do not claim live news, real-time alerts, real source retrieval, real AI analysis, or account sync.
- Add a feature graphic that does not contain small unreadable text or misleading non-Android device imagery.

## Policy Declarations

- Complete Data Safety from `docs/play_data_safety.md`.
- Complete target audience and content rating based on the final listing.
- Declare that the app has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no user-generated content for this release.
- Re-check declarations if any SDK, permission, backend, telemetry, or account behavior changes.

## Testing Tracks

- Upload the signed app bundle to internal testing first.
- Review install, launch, navigation, saved stories, settings, and story detail on at least one physical Android device.
- Review the Play pre-launch report and fix blocker warnings.
- If using a personal Play developer account subject to current rules, run closed testing with at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before applying for production access.
- Keep a summary of tester feedback and changes made for the production-access questionnaire.

## Production Rollout

- Submit only after policy declarations, privacy policy, release evidence, and pre-launch report are complete.
- Use staged rollout for the first production release.
- Monitor crashes, Android vitals, reviews, and support email after rollout.
- Halt or pause rollout if users report misleading content, launch crashes, saved-story data loss, or major layout/accessibility failures.
